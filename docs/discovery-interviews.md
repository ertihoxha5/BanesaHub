# M00.2 — Owner and property manager interviews

Status: Blocked on participant evidence. Interviews recorded: 0 of 3–5. Confirmations of a priority problem: 0 of at least 2.

Recruit 3–5 property owners or managers who handle rentals in Kosovo. Aim for a mix of small landlords and managers with several properties. Schedule 20–30 minutes each. Explain the purpose and ask permission to take anonymized notes. Record no names, addresses, tenant details, contracts, or payment records.

## Interview prompts

1. Si i menaxhoni sot pronat dhe qiratë? Më përshkruani procesin nga kontrata deri te regjistrimi i pagesës.
2. Më tregoni për rastin e fundit kur nuk ishte e qartë sa detyrohej një qiramarrës. Si e zgjidhët? Nëse kjo nuk ju ndodh, cili hap ju merr më shumë kohë?
3. Si e trajtuat kërkesën e fundit për mirëmbajtje? Si e dinit kush ishte përgjegjës dhe kur u mbyll?
4. Cili problem përsëritet më shpesh? Sa kohë ju merr dhe çfarë ndodh kur nuk zgjidhet?
5. Cilin problem do të zgjidhnit të parin dhe pse? Çfarë do të tregonte se procesi është përmirësuar?

Ask for recent examples before showing BanesaHub. Ask for frequency and impact without suggesting answers. Treat the PDF's problem statements as hypotheses until supported.

## Note template

Repeat once per participant: anonymous ID, date, role, portfolio size band, current tools and workflow, recent incident, frequency, impact, workaround, participant's top problem, and a measure of improvement. Note which details are estimates.

After the interviews, group recurring problems and link each conclusion to anonymized participant IDs and examples. Record conflicting evidence and distinguish what participants said from what the team inferred. To close this checkpoint, capture 3–5 interviews. The discovery acceptance gate also requires at least two real users to confirm a priority problem; leave it open if evidence is insufficient.
